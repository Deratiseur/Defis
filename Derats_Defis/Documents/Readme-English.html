Presentation


This module offers you around ten arenas to explore before you can reach the final arena and face Telim'Gar himself.

To get started, talk to the Challenge Master Golem on the bridge. It will offer to send you to ten arenas presented in order of (relative) difficulty, without telling you exactly what each one involves.

Each arena uses a new map taken from PST and has its own rules. In some of them, you will face drawbacks that are easy to handle; in others, they are downright vicious. If you think one of the challenges is impossible, keep this in mind: this module is merciless, so there is no point complaining.

There are several things you should know about the challenges:
  • Once you enter, you cannot leave until you find the Exit Rune (usually held by the boss, but not always).
  • The arenas are filled with monsters, each of which receives a random improvement. There are 58 possible improvements: some insignificant, others extremely dangerous. Pause the game and take a good look at what you are facing.
  • Monsters are immune to area-of-effect damage caused by other monsters and by traps.
  • You do not have to kill every monster to reach the boss. Sometimes the boss is already on the map. Sometimes it arrives later. Sometimes it is hidden and you have to find it... each map has its own rules regarding its boss.
  • In the Dump, you can find a Portable Portal which, while you remain inside the challenges (not outside them), can teleport you to a quiet room where you can rest, save, and prepare. Using the portal again returns you to the place you left.
  • Spellcasting monsters use the base game's vanilla scripts whenever possible so they can be enhanced by SCS.

Once you have successfully completed all ten challenges, the Challenge Master Golem will offer to let you meet its master: the final arena awaits you. Good luck.

Options


  • Option “Make the areas harder”: this option adds an original drawback to each area. Details of the additions are hidden in spoilers inside the area descriptions.
  • Options “Increase the number of random monsters placed” and “Random monsters drop random treasure”: these options can be installed or modified during an ongoing game. The changes will affect areas that have not yet been visited.

The Dump

Area statistics

  • Nothing special apart from two exits leading to other sub-areas.

Monsters appearing in the area

  • Otyughs
  • Mustard Jellies
  • Carrion Crawlers
  • Umber Hulks
  • Margoyles (4 APR; require a magical weapon to hit)
  • Ettins

Advice for players

  • The area is large and Proliferator Margoyles grow exponentially in number: find them before you are overwhelmed.
  • Otyughs cause disease, Mustard Jellies slow, Carrion Crawlers paralyze, and Umber Hulks cause confusion. Bring solutions/immunities for all of these.
  • The boss dispels and kills. Send in your tanks with good saving throws.

The Plant Labyrinth

Area statistics

  • Every 5 rounds, a portal appears at a random location for 5 rounds, accompanied by 1D3 random Carrion Crawlers. Taking the portal leads to the boss.
  • Every 1D4 rounds, each party member must make a Save vs. Spell at -2 or become entangled for 2 rounds.

Monsters appearing in the area

  • Grizzly Bears
  • Sword Spiders
  • Trolls
  • Basilisks (20% are Greater Basilisks)
  • Wyverns
  • Shambling Mounds
  • Carrion Crawlers

Advice for players

  • Bring protection against the petrifying Basilisks, and find the Proliferator Spiders quickly.
  • The portal leading to the boss is difficult to intercept and allows only one person through per interaction (you can interact with it several times before it moves). Whoever reaches the boss room is immediately swarmed by paralyzing Carrion Crawlers. Send someone with Free Action who can handle a large number of monsters.
  • Warning: when a character uses a portal and is teleported, they disappear from the screen. Pause immediately and click their portrait to find them again.
  • A trick for finding the portal easily: if cheats are enabled, open the map and press CTRL to display monsters (green circles). If you see a cluster of 3-4 small green squares, that is the portal (and its Carrion Crawlers).

Unique

The Mother of Crawlers:

  • Located off to the side in an area that is normally inaccessible (random portal to reach her)
  • Immune to acid
  • Immune to level 1-3 spells
  • Every 3 rounds, lays 1D3 random Carrion Crawlers
  • When struck, casts Absolute Immunity (once only)
  • Acidic melee backlash
  • Acidic, paralyzing bite (save at -4) that cuts in half (kills) creatures with fewer than 60 HP (save at -4).
If the challenges are made harder (option)

All Carrion Crawlers are infested with parasites -> in addition to their random improvement, they are all Swarm Masters. The boss has a 50% chance to cast Insect Plague when hit and casts Creeping Doom when she dies.

The Steel Tombs

Area statistics

  • Every round, a Cloudkill appears at a random location.
  • The area forms two loops; every exit leads to another exit, making it easy to lose pursuing monsters.
  • Four Greater Mummies are placed throughout the area. Once all four are dead, the boss appears somewhere in the area.

Monsters appearing in the area

  • Mummies
  • Skeleton Warriors
  • Bone Golems
  • Spectral Wizards
  • Helmed Horrors
  • Iron Golems

Advice for players

  • Hardened Iron Golems require a +4 weapon to hit.
  • Greater Mummies require a +3 weapon to hit, while Hardened Greater Mummies require a +5 weapon. Or kill them with spells.
  • The final boss is a nightmare for melee fighters and cannot be targeted by mages (permanent invisibility). Your best option is therefore tanks + ranged attackers, while your mages cast buffs or area spells that do not harm allies: Chain Lightning, Abi-Dalzim's Horrid Wilting, Creeping Doom, Dragon's Breath?
  • A trick for finding the boss easily: if cheats are enabled, open the map and press CTRL to display monsters (green circles). If you see one moving very quickly (Haste), that is the boss.

"Mulhorandi" spirits roam randomly throughout the area. If they spot monsters, they follow them. They are weak, but are Protectors, Temporals, and Leaders.

The Maze

Area statistics

  • Fully revealed from the start (the map is concave, so the whole area is visible).
  • Three "immortal" Raging Renders hunt you through the maze.
  • In the central area, every round: acid explosion for 4D4 acid damage (Save vs. Spell at -3 for half); if the saving throw fails, the victim is also stunned for 1 round.
  • Each acid explosion has a 16% chance to bring an Acid Paraelemental to life (not random).
  • Each portal sends you to the portal opposite it and has a 25% chance to generate a Portal Guardian (Invisible Stalker, not random) when you try to use it or when you arrive. The portal with no opposite counterpart sends you to the central area.
  • There is no boss to kill. All the players have to do is survive for 10 minutes.
  • The players do not arrive in a "safe area": monsters attack them as soon as they enter.

Advice for players

  • Mind Flayers and Beholders together, with nowhere to hide. You will have to rethink your strategy.
  • A good way to avoid the Raging Renders is to wait for them near a portal, let them approach, and then step through. The Portal Guardian will always be less troublesome than those three horrors.
  • Ocularons require a +3 non-crushing weapon to hit. Hardened Ocularons require a +5 non-crushing weapon. Or kill them with spells.

Monsters appearing in the area

  • Mind Flayers (20% are Ulitharids)
  • Beholders
  • Gauths
  • Ocularons (+3 weapon required to hit; immune to crushing damage and acid; True Sight; on hit: Save vs. Breath or blindness, while their poison-swollen eyes pop out and explode; poison cloud on death)
  • Otyughs (20% are Neo-Otyughs) (deadly poison)
  • Acid Paraelementals (in the center)
  • Portal Guardians (when using a portal)

Unique

3x Raging Renders:

  • Improved Haste, 6 APR (lore base Movement Rate: 30)
  • Regenerate 6 Hit Points per round
  • Damage: 2D10 + Strength bonus (19); 25% chance to deal double damage and kill the target on a failed Save vs. Death.
  • Immune to poison, mind-affecting effects, level drain, death effects, and petrification
  • If killed, it is recreated in the center of the area after 1D4+2 rounds.
If the challenges are made harder (option)
  • The portals no longer send the party to the opposite portal: they allow only one person through and send that character to another random portal or to the center. There is a 20% chance that no teleportation occurs and a Portal Guardian is created instead.
Antimaginium

Area statistics

  • Every round, each hero has a 20% chance of spellcasting failure and must make a Save vs. Spell at -2; on a failed save, spellcasting failure becomes 100%.
  • Beware of the very numerous traps!
  • The central altar can be activated to teleport the user (one person per use) to a random location. There is a 1-in-9 chance of being teleported into the boss's sealed area.

Advice for players

  • The challenge will be MUCH easier if your heroes are immune to electricity.
  • Magic Golems and Antimagic Quasielementals are immune to magical weapons. Make sure your heroes carry non-magical backup weapons.
  • The boss area has no exit: heroes teleported there cannot leave except by using the Exit Rune.
  • The boss disintegrates and drains levels. It can be harmed only by crushing weapons, electrical damage, and magical damage: keep that in mind when choosing the first character you send there.

Monsters appearing in the area

  • Nishruus
  • Vortex Spiders
  • Sojourner Shockers (magical weapon required to hit; electrical shield; attacks with 6D10 Chain Lightning at a cost of 8 HP) (yes, you can simply let it keep attacking until it eventually destroys itself).
  • Magic Golems (their attack drains 1 spell and 1 charge; immune to magical weapons and magic)
  • Antimagic Quasielementals (immune to magical weapons and magic; attack with 4 Magic Missiles that dispel on hit)

Unique

Living Black Blade of Disaster, animated +5 sword

  • 4 attacks per round
  • Each hit disintegrates the target on a failed Save vs. Death at +4
  • Each hit has a 10% chance to drain 4 levels from the target and restore 20 Hit Points to the Black Blade.
  • Immune to non-magical weapons
  • Immune to slashing and piercing damage
  • Immune to fire, cold, and acid
  • Partial resistances: crushing weapons 70%, electricity 70%, magic and magical damage 30%
  • Immune to mind-affecting effects, death effects, Intelligence drain, level drain, poison, disease, paralysis, and many other effects.
If the challenges are made harder (option)
  • Somewhere in the area, there is a space-time anomaly that slows down heroes and speeds up monsters approaching it. Every 3 rounds, a random monster from any other challenge emerges from this anomaly.

Area statistics

  • Every trap generates an Incendiary Cloud.
  • The two exits are interconnected: each sends you to the other.

Advice for players

  • The challenge will be MUCH easier if your heroes are immune to fire. This is especially true for your warriors when fighting the boss, and for your mages if they want to cast spells.

Monsters appearing in the area

The Tomb of the Great Wyrm

Area statistics

  • Every 1D6+2 rounds, an earthquake deals 4D6 crushing damage (Save vs. Spell for half). On a failed save: unconscious for 1 round, plus a 10% chance that an Earth Elemental appears (8/12/16/24 HD, random type and random improvement).
  • The boss is in a secondary area whose entrance remains blocked until you have killed 25 monster units, calculated as follows: 1 unit per Dragonborn/Half-Dragon, 3 units per Dragon.

Advice for players

  • The boss fight is basically "hit once, wait, hit once, wait." So if only one person should be attacking, it may as well be whoever hits hardest. While everyone else is "waiting," search the containers, set traps, and prepare for phase 2 of the fight.
  • Against the boss, your thief can hide at will and repeatedly backstab: the entire upper section of the staircase is outside the boss's field of vision, making it ideal for hiding again.
  • During phase 2 of the fight, the boss's companions drain two levels with every attack, with no saving throw:

If your heroes are not protected against level drain, they will very quickly die when their level reaches 0.

There is an Inquisitor who uses Dispel Magic: forget about the spell "Negative Plane Protection." Bring permanent protection from an item/ability, or kill the paladin very, very quickly.

If some heroes are not immune to level drain or are vulnerable to dispelling, leave them at the top of the stairs and have them use ranged weapons.

  • The boss does not drop the Exit Rune: you must retrieve it upstairs, in the dragon area. So as soon as you kill the boss, grab the drops and RUN!!!!!!!!!!!!! (Keep someone near the exit door so you can leave in one click.) Seriously, do not stay there!

Monsters appearing in the area

  • Dragonborn Smasher (Monk)
  • Green Half-Dragon (Cleric/Mage)
  • Red Half-Dragon (Fighter/Priest)
  • Green Dragon (less common)
  • Red Dragon (less common)
  • Earth Elementals

Unique

Priest, Archlich (in the secondary area)

  • Undead
  • Lich immunities
  • He has placed upon himself the same barrier that blocked the entrance: without a special event he is invulnerable (minimum Hit Points set to 1).
  • His cleric specialization allows him to cast Slay Living and Harm as ranged targeted spells.
  • Every time he is hit, he becomes protected by Sanctuary (from his equipment).
  • His items allow him to cast Iron Skins and Hardiness.
  • When reduced to 30% Hit Points, he casts Heal on himself.
  • When reduced to 30% Hit Points and he has no healing spell left, he calls upon his god for aid. Then the real fight begins:
  • The priest is fully restored and gains +200% Hit Points (but his god dislikes his cowardice and removes his protective barrier).
  • All his spells are restored and his items recharged.
  • He gains a casting-speed bonus: maximum Casting Time set to 4.
  • He receives 5 additional memorized Fingers of Death.
  • All his former companions are raised as Spectres (on hit, they deal 2 cold damage and drain two levels with no saving throw, in both melee and ranged combat).
  • When he dies, leave the area quickly. If curiosity makes you stay to see what happens, do not say I did not warn you.....

Miniature sand tornadoes wander randomly around the level (they are invulnerable). If a tornado passes near a hero, that hero is blinded for 1 round and must make a Save vs. Breath at -4 or be violently knocked back.

The Library (final arena)

Area statistics

  • A portal in one corner of the area produces a random Tarrasque every turn. It can be destroyed: 300 Hit Points, immune to all weapons and to every school of magic except Evocation and Conjuration; it can be damaged only by fire or electricity.
  • There is not one boss but three, each with a different specialty. Which one will you eliminate first?

Telim'Gar: an almost invulnerable Fighter/Mage who hits like a truck while casting spells instantly.

Grod One-Eye: an unapproachable undead warrior whose blows have irreversible consequences.

Dillpater: a mage who chains together near-instantaneous Imaskari spells and has effectively unlimited magic (as long as the potions he guzzles remain active).

Advice for players

  • Do not use the Portable Portal in this area.
  • The portal summons Tarrasques at regular intervals. You may want to deal with it before confronting the bosses.
  • Do not let Grod One-Eye hit you. And do not get close to him. Bombard him from a distance.
  • Against Dillpater, you have no chance without as much Magic Resistance and elemental resistance as possible.
  • A character protected from the Alteration school is protected from the "backlash" effects of Dillpater and Grod One-Eye.
  • If you deliberately let time pass, you can wait out Telim'Gar's protection from Divination and all his images, as well as the effects of Dillpater's potions.
  • If you know your Dungeons & Dragons lore, you probably already have a way to kill the Curst heroes in a single blow.

Monsters appearing in the area

  • 1 random Tarrasque, +1 each turn. Destroying the portal stops the flow. (550 Hit Points + Constitution 22, Fire Resistance 100%, Magic Resistance 35%, immune to Evocation spells, regenerates 3 Hit Points per round, +7 claws, increased critical range, devastating criticals)
  • Mithral Golems (Iron Golem immunities, Haste, 60% physical damage resistance)
  • Random Curst Heroes (Undead, AC +3, Magic Resistance 85%, high level with HLAs; they made it to the finale)

  • At the start of combat, he casts several buffs on himself, then Simulacrum, then Project Image. His Simulacrum benefits from the same buffs and also casts Project Image. That makes four Telim'Gars to deal with.

First lieutenant: Grod One-Eye, Death Knight (D&D 3rd Edition) (Fighter), level 35

If the challenges are made harder (option)

Random monster improvements


Monsters in the challenges all receive a random improvement. The categories below describe all 58 possible improvements.

Simple improvements
  • Accelerated: Haste.
  • Blurred: Armor Class +3 and saving throws +1.
  • Berserk: Permanent Berserk rage.
  • Furious: +3 attacks per round.
  • Powerful: Strength and Constitution set to 19.
  • Vital: Hit Points +200%.
  • Regenerating: Regenerates 3 Hit Points per round.
  • Precise: Critical-hit chance +4.
  • Refracted: 4 permanent Mirror Images.
  • Bloodbath: All damage dealt +200%.
  • Spitter: Uses ranged attacks instead of melee attacks; the Strength bonus is converted into ranged THAC0/damage bonuses.
  • Psychic: Uses ranged attacks instead of melee attacks; attacks explode around the target in a 2.5-meter radius and affect enemies only.
“On hit” abilities
  • Blinding: On hit, forces a saving throw or Blindness; on death, a 3-meter wave forces a saving throw at -4 or Blindness.
  • Venomous: On hit, forces a saving throw at -2 or Poison for 2 damage per second for 20 seconds (same as the Phase Spider).
  • Pestilent: Immune to disease; on hit, forces a saving throw at -3 or Disease: Slow, 1 damage/round, and all attributes -3, cumulative after 5 rounds.
  • Sundering: On hit, all saving throws and AC -1 and all resistances -5% for 1 turn, cumulative.
  • Cannibal: On hit, +1D4 “soul burn” damage and heals for the same amount.
  • Misfortune: On hit, all saving throws -1 for 1 turn, cumulative; on death, a 3-meter wave imposes -6 to all saving throws for 2 turns, cumulative.
  • Paralyzing: On hit, forces a saving throw at -2 or Hold Creature.
  • Brutal: +30% damage dealt; on hit, forces a saving throw or Stun for 1 round.
  • Destiny: Luck +4; on hit, Luck -1 for 1 turn, cumulative.
  • Vampiric: On hit, drains 2 levels.
  • Hypnotic: On hit, forces a saving throw or Dire Charm.
  • Killer: On hit, forces a saving throw at -2 or Death.
  • Void: On hit, forces a saving throw at -4 or Disintegration.
“When hit / on death” abilities
  • Noxious: When hit, 20% chance to release a small poisonous gas cloud. On death, automatically creates a 4.5-meter-radius cloud.
  • Cursed: On death, raises 10 ghostly replicas of itself.
  • Explosive: When hit, 25% chance to create a small 2.5-meter explosion for 4D6 damage (save for half); on death, a 4.5-meter explosion deals 10D6 damage (save at -2 for half).
  • Pyro-Bound: When hit, casts Incinerator for 3D8 damage on the attacker with no saving throw (no limit to the number of Incinerators in place).
  • Immortal: On its first death, it returns at full health and loses one level. On every later death, it loses two levels.
Recurring effects
  • Radiant: Every round, Radiation around it for 2D6 fire damage (save for half).
  • Howler: Every 3 rounds, Howl: save at -2 or Deafness, plus another save or Confusion.
  • Horrific: Every 3 rounds, Horror in an area. When hit, Haunting affects the attacker.
  • Necromantic: Every 2 rounds, creates a hasted Skeleton or Zombie, with a 4% chance to create a Skeleton Warrior or Ghoul instead.
  • Replicator: Every 2 rounds, creates a replica of itself lasting 6 rounds.
  • Proliferator: Every 4 rounds, creates a young version. The young creature becomes “normal” after 5 rounds and has a 33% chance to be a Proliferator itself. (Warning: this is exponential.)
Auras
  • Entropic: 4.5-meter aura, players only: Entropy.
  • Exhausting: 4.5-meter aura, players only: +1 Fatigue every second.
  • Intoxicating: 4.5-meter aura, players only: +1 Intoxication every second.
  • Silence: 4.5-meter aura, players only: Silence with no saving throw.
  • Siphoner: 4.5-meter aura, players only: forget 2 arcane spells every round.
  • Darkmoon: 4.5-meter aura, players only: Blindness and Deafness with no saving throw.
  • Leader: 4.5-meter aura, monsters only: THAC0, damage and saving throws +4, +1 attack per round.
  • Temporal: Immune to Slow and Time Stop, Improved Haste; 4.5-meter aura: Haste on monsters, Slow on players with no saving throw.
  • Protector: 4.5-meter aura, monsters only: minimum Hit Points set to 2.
  • Life-Giving: Regenerates 2 Hit Points per second; 4.5-meter aura, monsters only: regeneration 1 per second.
Combined improvements
  • Hardened: Armor Class +5, damage resistance +50%, weapon immunity increased by “+2” (no weapon immunity becomes immunity to normal and +1 magical weapons).
  • Pyrophoric: On hit, +1D10 fire damage; Fire Resistance 125%; Fire Shield; fire damage dealt +30%.
  • Cryogenic: On hit, +1D10 cold damage; Cold Resistance 125%; Cold Shield; cold damage dealt +30%.
  • Elder: +30 Hit Points, THAC0 +4, saving throws +2, caster level +4.
  • Antediluvian: +70 Hit Points, THAC0 +8, saving throws +5, +1 attack per round, caster level +10.
  • Charged: Electricity Resistance 125%, reflects electrical damage; when hit, Lightning Bolt 3D6 (save for half), with a 5% chance to cast Chain Lightning 6D6 (save for half) instead.
  • Shadow: Undead immunities, immune to non-magical weapons, drains 2 Strength on hit.
  • Swarm Master: Covered by an insect cloak (AC +2), reflects all insect spells; on hit or when hit, casts Summon Insects; on death, casts Insect Plague.
  • Antimagic: Immune to magic and magical weapons; 4.5-meter aura, players only: dead magic; on hit, dispels magic at 100% and applies a cumulative 25% spell-failure chance for 15 rounds.
  • Negative: On hit, drains 2 levels; when hit, drains 2 levels and deals +1D4 “soul burn” damage while healing for the same amount.
  • Super: +3 attacks per round, Improved Haste, +100% movement, elemental/physical resistances +80%, saving throws +3, physical attributes 23 (no extra Magic Resistance, so death/polymorph effects remain a way to get rid of it).
  • Scion of Magic: Improved Alacrity, maximum Casting Time 4, infinite spells.
